# Space Colony: Frontier - Control Scheme

## Desktop Controls
- **Camera Selection**: `1`-`5` or `C` to cycle through 5 camera modes.
- **Colony Overview**:
  - `W`/`A`/`S`/`D` or edge scroll: Pan camera
  - Right Mouse Drag: Orbit / rotate camera
  - Middle Mouse Drag: Fast pan
  - Mouse Wheel: Zoom in / out
  - Left Click: Select building / entity
  - `Delete` / `Backspace`: Demolish selected building (with confirmation)
- **Spaceship Flight**:
  - `W` / `S`: Forward / Reverse thrust
  - `A` / `D`: Strafe Left / Right
  - `Q` / `E`: Roll Left / Right
  - Mouse Pitch & Yaw: Flight steering
  - `Shift`: Boost thrusters (consumes extra fuel)
  - `Space`: Inertial brake / stabilization
  - `F`: Toggle Flight Assist (Auto-damp vs Newtonian drift)
  - `Left Click`: Fire mining laser / beam interact
- **Interface Shortcuts**:
  - `B`: Open Construction Menu
  - `T`: Open Research Tree
  - `M`: Open Planet Strategic Map
  - `J`: Open Mission Log
  - `Esc` or `P`: Pause Menu
  - `Space` (in colony view): Pause / Resume simulation time
  - `1`, `2`, `3` (in colony view): Simulation speed (1x, 2x, 5x)

## Mobile & Touch Controls
- **Virtual Left Joystick**: Direct movement and directional thrust.
- **Right Surface Touch-Drag**: Orbit camera / look direction.
- **Pinch to Zoom**: Camera zoom in/out.
- **Two-Finger Pan**: Pan colony view.
- **Context Buttons**: On-screen actions for Boost, Brake, Fire, and Docking.
